## Insights

- The completion audit needs two different evidence lanes: implementation contract evidence and real operating completion evidence.
- Development artifacts can prove that guardrails, UI contracts, and parser behavior exist, but they can create a false completion claim if they are treated as operating evidence.
- A machine-readable exclusion review is safer than prose-only policy because accepted gates can now verify the exclusion rule on every run.
- RTA-COMP-050 being proved means “byproduct exclusion is enforced in the audit path,” not “the RedTeam AX operational goal is complete.”
