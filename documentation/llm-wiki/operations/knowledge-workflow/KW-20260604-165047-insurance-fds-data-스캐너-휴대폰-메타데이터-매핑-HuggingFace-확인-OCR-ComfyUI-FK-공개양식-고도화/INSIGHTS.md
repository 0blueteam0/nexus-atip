## Insights
- HuggingFace has OCR/receipt/document-AI candidates but no direct Korean 실손보험 청구서류 dataset was found through tested queries. Use them for OCR/layout pretraining, not as Korean insurance ground truth.
- Scanner and smartphone submissions need separate metadata features: scanner dpi/page/PDF producer vs EXIF/device/capture-time/reupload-loss.
- High-value AF recipes are paired against a NO anchor: price tamper, diagnosis/KCD tamper, duplicate receipt reuse, overclaim line item insertion.
